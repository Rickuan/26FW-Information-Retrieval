9-11-2001 : Never Forget, Never Forgive




In loving memory of all those who died on that day.
God Bless you all. God Bless America.


I was so far away , but once it happened I felt something that I'll never forget.
The streets were empty, the people like astonished and frozen. And I saw it on their eyes: Terror.




I ask myself the meaning of all of this.
I ask myself why. Why to kill innocents.


Some years has gone ... and they are still killing.
Some years has gone ... and I know:
Whatever may happen. I'll never surrender.
Whatever will happen. I'll be ready and awake.


To defend my country and our freedom.
To defend the future of my family and the memory
of those who died.


This is my answer to your recent message, little porky coward named Osama Bin Laden. Do you really believe we are so afraid of you ?
